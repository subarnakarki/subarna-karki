/ 01 — ABOUT ME

About me

I'm a Software Development Engineer in Test. Most of my work is about making other engineers' jobs easier — test frameworks, developer tooling, and the automation that keeps CI honest. Over the past few years a lot of that has centered on figuring out where AI genuinely helps with testing and developer workflows, and where it just gets in the way.

I like work that's practical and holds up over time, and I'm usually happy to talk shop or pair on something.

What I care about

  • Building AI agents and developer tools that fix real, day-to-day engineering headaches.
  • Automation frameworks that teams actually want to use, not work around.
  • Finding where testing and AI overlap — and being honest about where they don't.
  • Writing things down and sharing what I figure out, often through open source.

What I work on

  • AI-assisted developer tools and automation.
  • Test frameworks — Playwright, Cypress, and API testing.
  • CI/CD pipelines and the day-to-day of keeping them fast.
  • Observability and quality engineering.
  • The occasional full-stack and cloud side project.

What I'm learning right now

Lately I've been going deeper on AI engineering and LLMs, agentic systems, and how they hold up in real distributed and cloud setups. On the side I follow quantitative trading, which scratches the same itch — building something that reacts intelligently without needing me to babysit it.

Away from the keyboard

  • Following the markets and tinkering with quant trading ideas.
  • Video games when there's time.
  • Traveling, and taking photos of wherever I end up.
  • Hanging out with my miniature dachshund, Momo.
  • And mostly, being around for my wife and our daughter, Saanvi — the hardest and best thing I've taken on.
