/ 01 — ABOUT ME

About me

I'm a Senior SDET focused on automation architecture, release confidence, and AI-assisted quality workflows. I was the sole QA engineer retained after Polly's 25+ person QA team was eliminated, owned quality end-to-end for ~50 developers, and shifted testing to them with Claude Code tooling, self-healing pipelines, and targeted PR validation. My work is less about writing tests for their own sake and more about creating systems that make quality scalable, measurable, and sustainable.

I like practical engineering: clear architecture, strong ownership, and a workflow that makes teams faster without creating noisy false confidence.

What I care about

  • Designing test systems that help teams move fast without creating release risk.
  • Automation frameworks that are maintainable, observable, and owned by the people using them.
  • Finding the real leverage points where AI improves testing — and where discipline matters more.
  • Turning quality work into a repeatable operating model rather than a last-minute scramble.

What I work on

  • Test automation architecture for distributed product teams.
  • Playwright, Cypress, and API testing strategy across full-stack product suites.
  • CI/CD quality gates, sharding, and targeted validation for faster merges.
  • AI-driven testing workflows, prompt-driven tooling, and self-healing automation.
  • Seed data, test infrastructure, and the operational systems behind reliable release confidence.

What I'm learning right now

Lately I've been going deeper on AI engineering and LLMs, agentic systems, and how they hold up in real distributed and cloud setups. On the side I follow quantitative trading, which scratches the same itch — building something that reacts intelligently without needing me to babysit it.
